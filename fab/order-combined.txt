Combined parts order -- 10 x logger + 5 x gate, +2 spare of each
==========================================================

Paste fab/order-combined-paste.txt into LCSC's quick-order
box, or upload order-combined.csv to their BOM tool.

Quantities are the build plus the spares, rounded up to
each part's real minimum from fab/lcsc-moq.csv. Anything
not in that table is left alone and LCSC raises it at
checkout. --no-moq turns the rounding off.

Spares are added BEFORE rounding, so on a 100-piece
minimum they cost nothing. They only change the lines
whose minimum is 1 or 5 -- which are the ones where
losing a part actually stops the build.

Buy elsewhere -- no LCSC part number
----------------------------------------------------------

  [logger] C6, C7       x20
        1 F 2.7 V low-ESR EDLC, 8 mm can, radial P3.5mm
        Eaton HB1030-2R5105-R or equivalent. TWO THINGS TO CHECK. (1) ESR must be under about 1 ohm: a 5.5 V coin-type EDLC is 30-200 ohm and cannot source the 120 mA this has to carry, so the rail would collapse the instant it was asked to. Cylindrical cells are tens of milliohms. (2) Anything from 0.1 F up works -- 0.1 F still gives about 500 ms of hold-up against the 2.5 s the design point buys

  [logger] J3, J4       x20
        0.1" pin SOCKET, 1x22 vertical
        RECEPTACLES, not pin headers: a DevKitC-1 ships with male pins already soldered pointing down, and it drops into these. Buy 1x22 strips -- most 1x40 socket strips do not cut cleanly and you lose a position

  [logger] J5, J7, J10  x30
        0.1" pin header, 1x04 vertical
        any

  [logger] J6           x10
        0.1" pin header, 1x03 vertical
        any

  [logger] harness      x10
        JST PH crimp housings + crimps
        PHR-4 x2 and PHR-10 x1 per board, plus SPH-002T-P0.5S crimps. Buy with the headers or the loom cannot be made up

  [logger] dev board    x10
        ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1, N8R8 or N16R8
        The MCU. Check the revision: v1.1 puts the onboard RGB LED on IO38, which this board leaves free for it. Some revisions use IO48, which is the WS2812 output here and would clash

  [gate] J2, J3       x10
        0.1" pin SOCKET strip, 1x07 vertical
        RECEPTACLES, not pin headers. The XIAO gets male pins soldered to its castellations pointing down and drops into these. Buy 1x07 strips or cut from 1x40 -- most socket strips lose a position when cut, so allow for that.

  [gate] J4           x5
        0.1" pin header, 1x04 vertical
        Any. The spare GPIO.

  [gate] H1-H4        x20
        M3 screw, nut and standoff
        4 corner holes, 3.2 mm.

  [gate] module       x5
        Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3
        The MCU. Fit the U.FL antenna that comes in the box -- a gate is the wrong place to rely on a chip antenna. It also needs male header pins soldered to its castellations, pointing DOWN, to plug into J2/J3.
