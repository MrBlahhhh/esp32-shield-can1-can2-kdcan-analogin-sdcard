2. Not available from LCSC/JLC -- source separately

   C6, C7       x10  1 F 2.7 V low-ESR EDLC, 8 mm can, radial P3.5mm
                Eaton HB1030-2R5105-R or equivalent. TWO THINGS TO CHECK. (1) ESR must be under about 1 ohm: a 5.5 V coin-type EDLC is 30-200 ohm and cannot source the 120 mA this has to carry, so the rail would collapse the instant it was asked to. Cylindrical cells are tens of milliohms. (2) Anything from 0.1 F up works -- 0.1 F still gives about 500 ms of hold-up against the 2.5 s the design point buys
